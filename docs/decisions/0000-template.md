# NNNN. Title in the imperative

Date: YYYY-MM-DD
Status: proposed | accepted | superseded by NNNN

## Context

What situation forces a decision. Two or three paragraphs at most. Link evidence.

## Decision

What we are doing, in one or two sentences. Then the specifics.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what we are giving up. Be honest about the second and third.

## Alternatives considered

Each with one sentence on why not.
